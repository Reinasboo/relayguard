Mezo Buildathon

Extend MUSD and MEZO. Build the Bitcoin Economy on Mezo, Ethereum, Base, and BNB Chain.


🎥 Start Here: Online Kickoff Workshop, October 13
About the Program

The Mezo Buildathon is a two-Wave builder program hosted on AKINDO for teams extending the utility of MUSD and MEZO: Mezo's Bitcoin-backed stablecoin and native token, built by Thesis, the team behind tBTC, Fold, and Taho.

The core requirement is simple: your project must integrate MUSD and/or MEZO. You can deploy on Mezo, Ethereum, Base, or BNB Chain.

This is not a one-shot hackathon. Teams build across two connected Waves, ship working products, get evaluated by the Mezo core team, and carry momentum forward. Wave 1 rewards early shipping. Wave 2 carries the larger pool and rewards depth.

$7,000 USD worth of MEZO tokens across two Waves, distributed on judged results. Direct access to the Mezo core team throughout.

The program runs on AKINDO. The conversation runs on Mezo's Discord. Join both. When you join the Mezo Discord, please mention that you're a participant in the Mezo Hackathon and would like to receive the Developer role and be added to the relevant channel.

What is Mezo?

Mezo is the Bitcoin finance app that lets people live off their Bitcoin without selling it. Borrow, earn, and spend, permissionlessly and without banks.

Two assets sit at the centre of this Buildathon:

MUSD: Mezo's native stablecoin, 100% backed by Bitcoin reserves and designed to hold 1:1 with the US dollar
MEZO: Mezo's native token

Your project needs to integrate one or both. What you build on top is open.

Mezo is EVM-compatible, so if you write Solidity, you can start today. The wider stack includes Bitcoin-collateralized borrowing, yield vaults, and the full Mezo app surface.

This program is built for mid-level and senior developers and teams with experience in:

EVM / Solidity development

Teams that already have a working product and want to add MUSD/MEZO integration, as well as teams creating a new project during the hackathon with a long-term commitment to it, are explicitly prioritized. So are new teams who can show a credible plan beyond the hackathon.

What to Build

Two tracks. Pick one, or submit to both.

Track 1: DeFi (Borrowing, Lending, Looping, and Yield)

How might we expand MUSD and MEZO utility as collateral and productive capital across DeFi?

Build protocols and strategies that make MUSD and MEZO a core building block in DeFi: composable collateral and yield-bearing capital.

Example projects:

Lending protocols accepting MUSD/MEZO as collateral, single-chain or cross-chain
Looping strategies for MUSD/MEZO positions (leveraged yield farming)
Yield-generating vaults and auto-compounding strategies for MUSD/MEZO
MUSD/MEZO liquidity pools and DEX integrations on other chains
Staking derivatives for MUSD/MEZO

Requirement: MUSD/MEZO integration.

Track 2: Access and Distribution for MUSD/MEZO

How might we make MUSD and MEZO easier to discover, access, and use?

Build the tools, bots, and interfaces that reduce friction for users interacting with Mezo's ecosystem, meeting users where they already are.

Example projects:

Telegram or WhatsApp bots for MUSD/MEZO transactions and monitoring
Matching and discovery platforms
Marketplaces and aggregators for Mezo ecosystem products
Cross-platform wallets and interfaces that simplify MUSD/MEZO access

Requirement: MUSD or MEZO integration.

What Mezo Is Looking For

These principles apply across both tracks and directly affect how you're scored.

Supported chains. You can deploy on Mezo, Ethereum, Base, or BNB Chain. Mezo testnet is welcome too.

Integration is mandatory. MUSD or MEZO integration is required. No integration, no eligibility.

Extend, don't detour. Projects that extend the reach and utility of existing Mezo products are prioritized over unrelated standalone apps.

Composability wins. Projects that plug into existing DeFi infrastructure (lending markets, DEXs, vote-escrow systems) score higher than isolated consumer apps.

Long-term commitment counts. Teams with working products who are adding MUSD/MEZO integration during the hackathon, or new teams who demonstrate a credible plan beyond it, are prioritized.

Rewards and Distribution

Total pool: $7,000 USD, paid in MEZO tokens.

Here is exactly how rewards work. No surprises.

How amounts are decided. After each Wave's judging period, the Mezo core team allocates points across eligible submissions. Each project's share of that Wave's pool is proportional to its share of total points. If a project earns 20% of the points, it receives 20% of the pool.

What you receive. Rewards are paid in MEZO tokens, distributed directly by Mezo. The MEZO amount is calculated using the 14-day time-weighted average price (TWAP) of MEZO on the day you complete KYB.

When you receive it. After the winner announcement, selected teams receive a KYB request from Mezo. Once you complete verification, Mezo will initiate payment on the same day if it is a working day. Otherwise, payment will be initiated on the first working day following completion.

KYB is required. Mezo conducts know-your-business verification on all grant recipients before disbursement. No KYB, no payout. Start preparing for this early if you're submitting as a team.

If you're notified as a winner. Mezo contacts potential winners by the email you provide at registration. Verification may include a W9 or W8-BEN form, government-issued ID, proof of address, and your wallet address. If you can't be contacted or don't pass verification, the prize is forfeited and awarded to the next-highest-scoring submission.

English version of the reply above, so the thread is readable for the judges and the rest of the wave.

1. ICR, liquidation price and the savings vault

Short answer: routing funds from the savings vault will not raise your borrowing level and will not improve your ICR.

Facts from the Mezo docs:
- collateral is BTC only (via tBTC); MUSD is the debt asset, not a collateral asset;
- a position is opened through BorrowerOperations.openTrove: you supply BTC, mint MUSD, and the BTC goes to ActivePool;
- sMUSD is the receipt token of the MUSD Savings Vault. Yield sits in the exchange rate, not in your balance. Depositing MUSD into the vault does not reduce the trove debt and does not add collateral.

So MUSD sitting in the savings vault is invisible to the lending protocol. A position improves only by adding collateral or repaying debt.

And separately, important: check the max borrow at transaction time, not once at open. The BTC price moves, and an amount computed at the old price can land below your target ratio when the tx executes.

2. Testnet or mainnet

It depends on the claim you are making. Testnet proves the mechanics work. Mainnet proves real users and real collateral. Say explicitly in the submission which one is shown. Do not mix them: testnet transactions presented as usage are the first thing a reviewer checks.

3. What to show for real users

A list of wallet addresses with transaction links. Unambiguously. A user count in an app cannot be verified; an address with openTrove or deposit in the explorer can. Three to five addresses with real transactions are stronger than a round number. If you have few users so far, say so.

4. The Vercel link

That was a real problem: the link opened someone else's app. Good that you are fixing it. Check every link before Wave 2 — one broken demo link devalues the rest.

And one thing that may matter more than anything else for your product.

If you are adding borrowing to an India-first savings app, note the floor: per the Mezo docs the minimum loan size is 1,800 MUSD, and with the 200 MUSD gas deposit total debt at opening is at least 2,000 MUSD. For goal savings in small amounts this constraint is stronger than ICR: a user saving toward 200–500 MUSD cannot open a loan at all.

We hit the same wall on our own testnet build and wrote it up in docs/MUSD_INTEGRATION_NOTES.md in the MezoGig repo, together with two other findings: MUSD uses 18 decimals on testnet, and the Savings Vault address listed in the docs has no code on chain 31611.

1. ICR, цена ликвидации и savings vault

Короткий ответ: маршрутизация из savings vault не повысит уровень заимствования и не улучшит ICR.

Факты из документации Mezo:
- залог — только BTC (через tBTC), MUSD — долговой актив, не залоговый;
- позиция открывается через BorrowerOperations.openTrove: вносишь BTC, выпускаешь MUSD, BTC уходит в ActivePool;
- sMUSD — receipt-токен MUSD Savings Vault. Доходность сидит в курсе обмена, а не в балансе. Депозит MUSD в vault не уменьшает долг по трогу и не добавляет залог.

То есть деньги в savings vault для протокола займов невидимы. Позиция улучшается только добавлением залога или погашением долга.

И отдельно важное: максимум займа надо проверять в момент транзакции, а не при открытии. Цена BTC двигается, и сумма, посчитанная по старой цене, на исполнении может дать ICR ниже порога.

2. Тестнет или мейннет

Зависит от того, какое утверждение делаешь. Тестнет доказывает, что механика работает. Мейннет — что есть реальные пользователи и реальный залог. В заявке надо прямо писать, что именно показано. Смешивать нельзя: тестнет-транзакции, поданные как использование, — первое, что проверят.

3. Что показать для реальных пользователей

Список кошельков со ссылками на транзакции. Однозначно. Количество пользователей в приложении проверить нельзя; адрес с openTrove или deposit в обозревателе — можно. Три-пять адресов с настоящими транзакциями убедительнее круглой цифры. Если пользователей пока мало — так и напиши.

4. Ссылка Vercel

Это была реальная проблема: по ссылке открывалось чужое приложение. Хорошо, что исправляешь. Проверь все ссылки перед вторым этапом — одна битая ссылка на демо обесценивает остальное.

И одно, что может оказаться важнее всего для твоего продукта.

Если ты добавляешь заимствование к копилке для Индии, учти минимум: по документации Mezo минимальный размер займа — 1 800 MUSD, и вместе с 200 MUSD газового депозита общий долг при открытии — не меньше 2 000 MUSD. Для целевых накоплений на небольшие суммы это ограничение сильнее, чем ICR: пользователь, который копит на 200–500 MUSD, кредит открыть не сможет.
