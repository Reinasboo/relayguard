TOPICS

Built for MezoLive product on Testnet. No automatic borrowing.

01What is RelayGuard?

RelayGuard is a MUSD commerce and payment-control product built for Mezo. Merchants can create shareable invoices for people, while software agents can pay to unlock a protected API resource. A policy console adds spending rules and settlement evidence.

02How does customer checkout work?

A merchant creates an exact MUSD invoice with an expiry and shares its link or QR code. The customer connects a Mezo-compatible wallet, reviews the transfer, and signs it. RelayGuard checks the Mezo receipt and matching MUSD transfer before the invoice is marked paid.

Invoice→Wallet signs→Receipt verified
03Does RelayGuard hold or custody customer funds?

No. The buyer signs a direct MUSD transfer to the merchant’s configured receiving wallet. RelayGuard records invoice and verification state; checkout funds do not pass through a RelayGuard custody account.

04What does x402 do in this product?

x402 lets an agent request a protected HTTP resource and receive exact MUSD payment terms when payment is required. The buyer signs a payment authorization; the Mezo facilitator verifies and settles it. The API releases the resource only after successful settlement.

05Who pays gas for an agent payment?

The facilitator handles gas for the MUSD settlement. If the wallet first needs to approve an exact Permit2 allowance, that approval is a separate transaction and uses BTC for gas on Mezo. RelayGuard does not request an unlimited token approval.

06How does the trove-aware spending policy work?

Before an operator-authorized MUSD agent payment, RelayGuard reads Mezo trove health and applies an application policy: at or above 150% ICR, configured limits apply; from 110% to below 150%, human approval is required; below Mezo’s 110% minimum, new MUSD authorization is blocked. If protocol data cannot be read, MUSD policy actions fail closed. The 150% buffer is RelayGuard’s choice, not a Mezo parameter.

07Does every buyer need a trove? Does RelayGuard change one?

No. A buyer can pay from an existing MUSD balance without a trove. If the connected wallet is short on MUSD and has no active trove, checkout offers an optional Mezo Testnet-only BorrowerOperations flow. It shows the live minimum borrow, fee, fixed simple interest rate, gas compensation, BTC collateral, oracle price, estimated collateral ratio, and gas before the buyer separately confirms in their own wallet. This creates debt and may expose BTC to liquidation; RelayGuard never opens a trove or pays the invoice automatically. Wallets with an existing trove are directed to Mezo’s official app to manage that position.

08What evidence is recorded for a policy decision?

Mezo reads for a decision are pinned to one block. The authorization retains the borrower, block, observation time, price, ICR, collateral, debt, and risk tier. Hash-linked audit events commit policy and evidence digests, so edits to stored evidence can be detected during verification.

09What happens if the trove changes after approval?

RelayGuard rechecks the policy before approval and execution. If the risk tier changed, a stale approval or execution is rejected; the operator can cancel the pending authorization and submit a fresh request. An authorized human can still reject a stale request.

10Is RelayGuard production-ready for mainnet?

RelayGuard is currently a Mezo Testnet demo (chain ID 31611), not a mainnet payment service. Testnet invoice and x402 settlements have been verified. Controlled live testing of policy thresholds, a fresh production QR checkout, and operational hardening remain outstanding. Do not send mainnet funds to this demo.

11Whose wallet balance and trove do I see in the payment workspace?

These are the balances and trove for RelayGuard’s operator-configured signer. They are not your connected wallet, your signed-in account, or a merchant’s payout wallet. The status dashboard is public and read-only; payment controls require sign-in.

12Can my business use its own wallet and checkout?

Yes. The merchant workspace is multi-tenant: each signed-in merchant can verify its own receiving wallet and create invoices that pay that wallet directly. Merchant profiles, invoice history, and x402 resources are scoped to the merchant account; the operator balance shown on this payment dashboard is separate.

13What can I do here without signing in?

Anyone can inspect the operator signer’s public Mezo Testnet balance and trove status. Creating payment requests, viewing protected spending limits and recipient settings, and approving or executing payments require an authenticated account. The separate merchant checkout flow remains available to buyers without a RelayGuard sign-in.

14Can I choose which wallet connects?

Yes. When multiple browser wallets are detected, choose the one to use for that action. RelayGuard uses that wallet for the Mezo Testnet connection and never accesses your private key; you review and approve wallet prompts yourself.

15Can I integrate RelayGuard’s SDK into my own website?

Yes. Use RelayGuard’s Express x402 SDK/adapter in your Node.js API to protect a route on your own site or service. You keep your storefront, server, and response; confirmed MUSD payments settle to your verified wallet. It is currently provided as source code to copy into your project—not as a published npm package or a drop-in browser checkout widget. Hosted invoice links and QR checkout are also available.
