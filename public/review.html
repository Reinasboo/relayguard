CONTROLLED SCENARIOS · NOT LIVE TROVE STATES

110% protocol minimum. 150% app review buffer.
The protocol minimum is a liquidation boundary. RelayGuard adds an earlier review threshold for the configured operator’s treasury. Paying a merchant does not itself change trove debt or collateral. Buyer checkout uses separate wallet and balance checks.
